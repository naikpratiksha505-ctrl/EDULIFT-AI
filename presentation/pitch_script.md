# EduLift AI — 60 to 90-Second Hackathon Pitch Script

*Target Speaking Duration: 75 seconds (~180–210 words delivered at a confident, conversational pace).*

---

### [0:00 - 0:15] The Hook & Problem
"Good morning, judges and fellow builders!

Every semester, thousands of first-year engineering students struggle in silence. By the time final exam grades reveal who needed help, the semester is already over. 

Existing student management systems are passive digital ledger books: they record poor attendance and failing marks, but they never offer a pathway forward."

### [0:15 - 0:40] The Solution & Philosophy
"That is why we built **EduLift AI** — an AI-powered student analytics and success platform rooted in one fundamental belief: **Every student deserves a chance to succeed.**

EduLift AI replaces punitive labels with constructive guidance. When a student's attendance drops or a quiz mark dips, our system doesn't flag them as a failure. Instead, it provides actionable, empathetic recommendations explaining *why* the indicator appeared and *what* step they can take today."

### [0:40 - 1:05] Key Capabilities & Technology
"On the student side, EduLift AI pairs performance analytics with an automated Weekly Study Planner that creates balanced 45-minute focus sessions with built-in breaks based on the student's actual availability.

On the faculty side, mentors receive early support indicators across their cohort, enabling them to record 1-on-1 check-ins, tutoring plans, and follow-up outcomes.

Behind the scenes, we combine transparent rule-based logic with a scikit-learn machine learning demonstration trained to explain feature impact — all built with Python, Flask, SQLite, and Chart.js."

### [1:05 - 1:20] Closing Impact
"EduLift AI bridges the gap between academic data and human mentorship — turning early warnings into student triumphs. 

Thank you, and we invite you to explore our live demo!"
